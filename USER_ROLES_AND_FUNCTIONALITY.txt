=============================================================================
DEALFLOW360 — USER ROLES & FUNCTIONALITY REQUIREMENTS
Based on System Architecture Diagram
=============================================================================

There are 5 types of users in the DealFlow360 ecosystem.
Each has a different role, different access, and different set of screens.

=============================================================================
1. SALES REPRESENTATIVE
=============================================================================
App Used: Internal Sales App

What they do day-to-day:
- They talk to customers and build quotations for them.
- They have NO power to approve a quote themselves.
- They track deals and check the health of their pipeline.

=============================================================================
HOW THE SYSTEMS CONNECT (Data Flow Summary)
=============================================================================

STEP 1: Admin sets up the rules
         Admin Panel → Products, Discount Rules, Approval Rules, Price Lists

STEP 2: Sales Rep creates a quote
         Internal Sales App → Quotation Builder → Discount Engine runs automatically
         → Risk Score is calculated → Quote submitted for Approval

STEP 3: Approval Chain runs
         Sales Manager reviews → If high risk, escalates to Finance
         → Approved or Rejected with comments

STEP 4: Customer receives the quote
         Customer Portal → Customer views quote → Can negotiate / counter-offer
         → If negotiated, Re-Approval Trigger fires inside the Sales App

STEP 5: Customer accepts
         Customer clicks Confirm → Order is created automatically
         → Inventory is checked and stock is allocated
         → Fulfillment begins, invoice is generated

STEP 6: Finance closes the deal
         Invoice sent → Customer pays → Finance marks as Paid
         → Deal Health Engine closes the deal record

=============================================================================
WHAT WE BUILT IN THIS HACKATHON:
=============================================================================
We built STEP 1 entirely — the Admin Panel.
This is the foundation layer that the entire DealFlow360 ecosystem runs on.
Without the Admin Panel correctly configuring users, products, rules, and 
warehouses — none of the other applications can function.

The Admin Panel is the backbone. We built the backbone.
